# CampusMate demo script

## Registration and sign-in

On a fresh install, create an account with a valid name, numeric SAP ID, `@nmims.in`-formatted email, STME/SOC/SOL department, year 1–5, and matching password fields. The app saves a device-local profile and salted password verifier, then signs in. Sign out and sign in again using SAP ID, the same email, and password.

The email suffix is a format check only. NMIMS does not verify the identity or email ownership, and account/data do not sync to a server. Password reset is unavailable offline because SAP ID and email alone do not prove email ownership.

## Local workflows

1. **Attendance:** create/select a semester, add subjects, log Present/Absent/Cancelled sessions, and use per-subject Attend/Miss plus/minus controls. Confirm the personal-record notice and department advisory target.
2. **Assignments:** add an assignment with a current-semester subject and due date, toggle completion, and delete it.
3. **Campus:** view the NMIMS Chandigarh location, open directions, and open the NMIMS Chandigarh website.
4. **Profile:** view/edit the registered local profile and choose a photo.
5. **Resources:** open the bundled NMIMS Chandigarh information links.

Attendance, assignments, and profile data are stored on the device. They are not university records and do not sync across devices.
